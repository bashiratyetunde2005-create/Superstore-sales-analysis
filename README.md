# Superstore-sales-analysis
## Project Overview

We were approached by a national retail company to analyze its sales performance using a Superstore sales dataset.

The purpose of this project was to evaluate the company's overall sales and profitability, identify high- and low-performing regions and product categories, understand customer segment performance, analyze sales trends over time, and provide actionable recommendations to support better business decisions.

The analysis was conducted using Power BI, with the results presented through an interactive dashboard.

## Business Problem

The company generates significant sales across different regions, customer segments, and product categories. However, high sales do not always translate into high profitability.

Management therefore needs to understand which areas of the business are performing well, which areas require attention, and what factors may be contributing to differences between sales and profit.

The analysis aims to provide management with data-driven insights that can help improve profitability, identify business risks, and support better resource allocation.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed to answer the following business questions:

1. What is the overall sales performance of the company?
2. Which regions generate the highest sales and profit?
3. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?
4. Which product categories perform best?
5. Which products are the most profitable?
6. What trends can be observed over time?
7. What recommendations can management implement to improve business performance?

---

## Dataset

The analysis uses a Superstore sales dataset containing information about sales transactions, including:

* Order information
* Customer information
* Product information
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity
* Discount
* Region
* Customer segment
* Product category
* Order date

> **Note:** The dataset is embedded in the Power BI project file and was used as the source for the analysis.
